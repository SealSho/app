1. Information Collected and Usage
This App collects user information through the following third-party services:

Firebase Analytics: Used to collect usage statistics and analyze user behavior.

Sentry: Used to collect error and crash reports to assist in resolving technical issues.

AI Service Providers (e.g., Anthropic / Claude): Code or data inputs may be processed via AI services solely for providing functional features or assisting in code debugging.

This App does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, contact information, or government ID. In the event of a crash, raw diagnostic data and error logs are collected solely for fixing bugs.

This App does not collect detailed payment or subscription information; store receipts and validation data are verified locally on your device.

This App collects device time and server-verified time for functional validation and analytical purposes. Device time is never uploaded directly to servers and is used only for anonymous event logging.

2. Purpose of Use
The collected information is used strictly for the following purposes:

To provide and maintain the features and services of this App;

To analyze user interactions and optimize product experience;

To locate, diagnose, and fix software defects and performance issues.

3. Security Measures
To safeguard user information, the developer implements security management measures including, but not limited to:

Establishing and enforcing strict privacy protection policies;

Restricting access to collected data exclusively to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis;

Conducting regular privacy and security awareness training;

Implementing technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or alteration of data.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties
The developer will not provide or transfer user information to third parties without prior user consent, except as required by laws and regulations.

The following situations do not constitute unauthorized third-party disclosure:

Providing necessary information to third-party service providers performing essential tasks on our behalf (e.g., operators of Firebase, Sentry, and integrated AI services);

Disclosing information in compliance with legal obligations, court orders, or government administrative demands;

Transferring information in connection with a business merger, acquisition, or asset transfer.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
The developer reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as necessary. Any changes will be posted within the App with an updated effective date. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

6. Data Deletion Requests
If a user requests the deletion of their data, the developer will process the request promptly in accordance with the standard data deletion procedures provided by Firebase, Sentry, and related service providers.

7. Anonymous Statistics and Identification
All information collected via Firebase Analytics and Sentry consists of aggregated, anonymous statistics that cannot be used to directly identify any individual user.

8. International Data Transfers
User information may be transferred to and processed on overseas servers operated by our service providers (e.g., Firebase and Sentry). All service providers adhere to internationally recognized privacy protection standards (such as GDPR).

9. Protection of Minors
This App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the legal minimum age in your jurisdiction). Users must be of legal age or have obtained consent from a parent or legal guardian to use this App.

By using this App, users (including minors) represent that they have obtained appropriate guardian consent.
